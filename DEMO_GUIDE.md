# Demo Walkthrough (all data synthetic)

1. **Overview** – system architecture and team responsibilities.
2. **Live Risk Monitor** – pick a simulated trip, press Play. Behaviour, alcohol and road context combine into one risk score with alerts. Use the alcohol presets to trigger a High-risk alert.
3. **Driver Behaviour** – replay of a simulated trip: model output against ground truth.
4. **Alcohol Detection** – move the sensor sliders to see the impairment probability.
5. **Crash Severity** – set road, weather and vehicle conditions and predict risk and severity.
6. **Hotspots and Safe Routes** – click the map to set start and end; adjust the safety weight to compare fastest and safest routes.
