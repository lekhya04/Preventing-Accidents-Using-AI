Predict the risk.

SafeDrive AI fuses how the driver drives, whether the driver is impaired and how dangerous the road conditions are into one real-time risk score, then alerts the driver and recommends a safer route.

Lekhya · Sub-area 1

Driver Behaviour Detection

Smartphone IMU signals → sliding windows → time & FFT features → RBF-SVM / Random Forest. Evaluated leave-one-trip-out on a real labelled driving dataset.

Srinivas · Sub-area 3

Alcohol Impairment Detection

Cabin MQ-3 alcohol concentration, thermal face temperature and pupil ratio → impairment classifier.

Lekhya · Sub-area 2

Crash Risk & Accident Severity

Three model families from the literature on 314,285 real accident records: the standard paper's ensemble comparison, the Enhanced CCP tree (Paper 3) and the Multi-Task FT-Transformer (Paper 4).
