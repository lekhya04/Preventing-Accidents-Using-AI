# Form text for IEEE DataPort - SafeDrive-Sim-IMU

**Title:** SafeDrive-Sim-IMU: A Synthetic Smartphone Inertial-Sensor Dataset of Driving Events (Aggressive Manoeuvres, Speed Bumps and Potholes)

**Authors:** Lekhya [surname], Srinivas [surname], Likhitha [surname]  –  Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham

**Keywords:** synthetic data; driver behaviour; smartphone sensors; accelerometer; gyroscope; aggressive driving; road safety; time series

**Abstract (paste):**
SafeDrive-Sim-IMU is a fully synthetic dataset of smartphone inertial-sensor recordings for driving-event detection. It contains 12 simulated car trips (about 10 minutes each) with accelerometer, linear-acceleration and gyroscope signals at about 50 Hz and 184 labelled events in nine classes: aggressive braking, aggressive acceleration, aggressive left/right turns, aggressive left/right lane changes, non-aggressive (gentle) manoeuvres, speed bumps and potholes. Trips were generated by a physics-inspired, seeded simulator with three simulated driver profiles, two vehicle types, city and highway roads, dry and wet surfaces, realistic sensor noise, phone-mount misalignment, timestamp jitter, dropped samples and annotation noise. Aggressive and gentle intensity ranges deliberately overlap so that classification is non-trivial. No real vehicles or people were recorded; the dataset is intended for prototyping, education and benchmarking of driver-behaviour algorithms, and the generator code is included for full reproducibility.

**Instructions (paste):**
Unzip SafeDrive-Sim-IMU_data.zip. Each trip folder holds accelerometer.csv, linear_acceleration.csv, gyroscope.csv, ground_truth.csv and trip_info.json; trips_metadata.csv and events_all.csv are at the top level. Axes: x right, y forward, z up. Event start/end are in seconds from trip start (convert uptimeNanos with (uptimeNanos - first uptimeNanos)/1e9). See the documentation PDF. To regenerate: python generate_driver_behavior.py --seed 2026.
