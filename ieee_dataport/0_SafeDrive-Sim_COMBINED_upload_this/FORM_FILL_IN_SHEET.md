## Abstract (paste)
SafeDrive-Sim is a fully synthetic, openly documented multi-source dataset for driver-risk research. It bundles three linked parts generated by seeded simulators: (1) 12 simulated car trips with smartphone accelerometer, linear-acceleration and gyroscope signals at about 50 Hz and 184 labelled events in nine classes (aggressive braking, acceleration, turns and lane changes, gentle manoeuvres, speed bumps and potholes); (2) 2,000 simulated driving sessions from 60 simulated participants with five in-cabin sensor features (gas, cabin and facial temperature, pupil ratio) labelled alcohol / no alcohol; and (3) 30,000 simulated road-crash records for a Coimbatore-area bounding box with weather, road, vehicle and time context and three outcomes (severity, road length affected, clearance time). Class overlap, sensor noise, confounders and participant-level effects are built in so that the tasks are non-trivial. No real vehicle, person, sensor or crash was recorded; crash locations do not correspond to real accident black spots. The data are intended for prototyping, teaching and benchmarking of driver-behaviour, impairment and crash-severity models and of fusion systems; generator scripts are included so every file can be regenerated exactly.
